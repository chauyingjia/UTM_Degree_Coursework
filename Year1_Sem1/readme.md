## 💡 About This Semester

Year 1 Semester 1 was my first step into the Computer Science degree journey. This semester introduced me to the basic foundations of computing, programming, mathematics, digital systems, and university-level learning. Since everything was still new, it took time for me to adjust to the workload and understand how each subject connected to the field of computer science. Even though it was challenging at first, this semester helped me build confidence and gave me a clearer direction for my studies.

## 🧮 <ins><a href="https://github.com/chauyingjia/Year1_sem1/tree/main/SECI1013%20DISCRETE%20STRUCTURE">Discrete Structure</a></ins>

### Course Overview

Discrete Structure introduced mathematical concepts that are important in computer science. The course covered topics such as logic, sets, relations, functions, counting, graphs, and other mathematical foundations used in computing. It helped develop logical thinking and problem-solving skills.

### Reflection

This course was quite challenging because it required a different way of thinking compared to normal mathematics. Some topics felt abstract at first, but they became more meaningful when I understood how they relate to programming and computer science. It helped me improve my logical reasoning and gave me a stronger foundation for future technical subjects.

## 💻 <ins><a href="https://github.com/chauyingjia/Year1_sem1/tree/main/SECJ1013%20PROGRAMMING%20TECHNIQUE%20I">Programming Technique I</a></ins>

### Course Overview

Programming Technique I introduced the basic concepts of programming using C++. The course covered variables, data types, control structures, functions, arrays, and problem-solving techniques. It focused on building the foundation needed to write simple and structured programs.

### Reflection

This was one of the most important courses in my first semester because it introduced me to actual programming. At first, writing code felt confusing, especially when dealing with errors and logic problems. However, through practice, I slowly became more comfortable with programming. This course helped me understand that coding is not only about syntax, but also about learning how to think step by step.

## 🌐 <ins><a href="https://github.com/chauyingjia/Year1_sem1/tree/main/SECP1513%20TECHNOLOGY%20AND%20INFORMATION%20SYSTEM">Technology and Information System</a></ins>

### Course Overview

Technology and Information System introduced the role of information systems and technology in modern organisations. The course covered topics related to computer systems, networks, databases, software, and current technology trends. It also helped students understand different career paths and real-world applications in the computing field.

### Reflection

This course helped me see the bigger picture of computer science beyond just programming. Through this subject, I learned more about how technology is used in industries and how different computing areas are connected. It also helped me understand the importance of staying updated with technology trends as a computer science student.

---

## 🔌 <ins><a href="https://github.com/chauyingjia/Year1_sem1/tree/main/SECR1013%20DIGITAL%20LOGIC">Digital Logic</a></ins>

### Course Overview

Digital Logic focused on the basic principles behind digital systems. The course covered binary numbers, logic gates, Boolean algebra, combinational circuits, sequential circuits, and how digital components work together. It helped explain how computers process information at a low level.

### Reflection

Digital Logic was interesting because it showed what happens behind the scenes inside computer hardware. Before this course, I only understood computers from the software side. Learning about logic gates and circuits helped me understand how simple binary operations can form the foundation of computer systems. It made me appreciate how hardware and software are connected.

## 🛡 <ins><a href="https://github.com/chauyingjia/Year1_sem1/tree/main/ULRS1032%20INTEGRITY%20AND%20ANTI-CORRUPTION">Integrity and Anti-Corruption</a></ins>

### Course Overview

Integrity and Anti-Corruption focused on ethical values, responsibility, honesty, and the importance of preventing corruption. The course discussed real-life situations where integrity plays an important role in personal, academic, and professional life.

### Reflection

This course was different from the technical subjects, but it was still meaningful. It reminded me that knowledge and skills should be used responsibly. As a student and future professional, integrity is important because trust and accountability matter in every field, including technology and data-related work.

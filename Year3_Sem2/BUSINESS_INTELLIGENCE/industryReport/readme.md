
# 🗣️ Industry Talk Reflection: Data Governance & Data Management in Business Intelligence

## Talk Overview

The industry talk was titled **“Bridging Data & Decisions: The Role of Data Governance and Data Management in Modern Business Intelligence.”**

The session explained how modern organizations use data governance and data management to support trusted Business Intelligence and decision-making. It also showed that BI is not only about dashboards, but also about making sure the data behind the dashboard is reliable, consistent, and well-managed.

## What I Gained

Through this talk, I learned that many real organizations face problems such as messy data, inconsistent definitions, missing documentation, and unclear data ownership. These problems can affect the accuracy of reports and dashboards.

I also learned that data governance is important because it helps organizations create trusted data. Concepts such as **Master Data Management (MDM)**, **data cataloging**, **data lineage**, **data quality**, and **Role-Based Access Control (RBAC)** were introduced during the talk.

## Reflection

This talk gave me a clearer view of how BI is used in real organizations. In class, BI may seem like a process of cleaning data and creating dashboards, but in the industry, the process is more complex.

Before business users can trust a dashboard, the data must be properly governed, documented, cleaned, and protected. This helped me understand that BI professionals need both technical skills and strong business understanding.

## Overall Learning

Overall, this industry talk was valuable because it connected classroom learning with real industry practice. It helped me realize that successful BI implementation depends on data quality, governance, communication, and clear decision-making support.


# 📊 Business Intelligence Reflection

## Course Overview

This course introduced the main concepts and practical skills of Business Intelligence. Throughout the course, I learned how raw data can be prepared, transformed, analysed, and presented as useful insights for business decision-making.

The course provided hands-on experience with **Alteryx Designer** and **Microsoft Power BI**. Alteryx was used for data cleaning, transformation, and workflow design, while Power BI was used to create interactive dashboards and visual reports.

## Reflection

This course helped me understand that Business Intelligence is not only about creating dashboards, but also about preparing reliable data before analysis. The lab exercises and project showed me how important data quality, workflow design, and clear visualization are in real BI work.

Learning Alteryx Designer helped me understand the backend process of BI, especially how data can be cleaned and joined through structured workflows. Power BI helped me learn how insights can be presented in a clear and interactive way for business users.

## Overall Learning

Overall, this course improved my understanding of how data supports business decisions. It also strengthened my skills in data preparation, ETL workflow design, dashboard development, and business reporting.

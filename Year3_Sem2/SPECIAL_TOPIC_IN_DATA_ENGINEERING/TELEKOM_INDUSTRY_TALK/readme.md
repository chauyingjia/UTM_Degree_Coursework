## 🏢 Reflection: Telekom Malaysia Industry Talk

On the 20th of May, I attended an industry talk by Mr. Zaid Waqi Zulkifli from TMOne Malaysia. This session gave me a clearer understanding of how enterprise data systems work in large organizations and how they are often more complex than what is usually learned in the classroom.

### 💡 What I Gained

I learned that real-world enterprise systems are not always simple, clean, or perfectly structured. Large organizations need to manage many systems, departments, business processes, regulations, and customer requirements at the same time.

One of the key lessons I gained was that **business changes faster than systems**. Businesses may change due to new products, customer demands, competition, regulations, or acquisitions. However, enterprise data systems usually require more time to update, maintain, and integrate.

### 🧩 Enterprise System Experience

The example of a small nasi lemak business growing into a more complex operation helped me understand how business growth can increase system complexity. As a business expands, more systems are needed to manage sales, inventory, customers, suppliers, and financial records.

The session also introduced enterprise systems such as SAP, Oracle, and HubSpot. This helped me realize that data engineers must not only understand technical tools, but also how business operations work.

### 🔧 Suggested Improvements

To prepare better for this kind of industry environment, I should improve my understanding of enterprise systems, data integration, and business workflows. Besides that, communication skills should also be strengthened because data professionals often need to work with clients, users, and stakeholders.

### 🌟 Overall Reflection

Overall, this industry talk was meaningful because it connected technical knowledge with real business situations. It helped me understand that data engineering is not only about building systems or pipelines, but also about solving business problems and supporting better decision-making.

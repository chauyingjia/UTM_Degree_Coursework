## 📊 Reflection: Tutorial 5 - BI Dashboard Article

Through this tutorial, I gained a better understanding of how Business Intelligence can help organizations monitor performance and support decision-making. The tutorial was based on a case study of PT. MPM, a packaging manufacturing company that needed a better way to monitor sales and production performance.

### 💡 What I Gained

I learned that BI dashboards are useful when companies have scattered data sources and manual reporting processes. By applying an ETL process and storing cleaned data in a centralized data warehouse, business users can access more accurate and meaningful insights.

This tutorial also helped me understand the design of a BI system from different perspectives, including user stories, ERD, use case diagram, system architecture, and star schema design. I learned how sales, production, inventory, machine performance, and customer data can be connected to support dashboard reporting.

### 📈 BI & Data Modelling Experience

I gained a better understanding of how dashboards should be designed based on user needs. For example, top management needs KPI summaries and performance trends, while data analysts need clean and structured data for reporting. The star schema design also helped me understand how fact and dimension tables can support faster analytical queries.

### 🔧 Suggested Improvements

The system could be improved by adding real-time data updates, more interactive dashboard filters, and predictive analytics for sales forecasting and production planning. The dashboard could also include automated alerts when production efficiency or inventory levels fall below a certain threshold.

### 🌟 Overall Reflection

Overall, this tutorial was valuable because it showed how data engineering, database design, and business intelligence are connected. It helped me understand that dashboards are not only visual tools, but also decision-support systems that help organizations improve efficiency, monitor performance, and respond faster to business problems.

## 🎨 Reflection: PPG Industry Visit

On the 7th of April, I had the opportunity to join an industry visit to PPG. This visit was especially meaningful because it allowed me to physically experience the company’s office and working environment after previously attending industry talks related to PPG.

### 💡 What I Gained

Through this visit, I gained a better understanding of PPG as a global company that focuses on paints, coatings, and specialty materials. I also learned that although PPG is involved in manufacturing, technology and data play an important role in supporting its global business operations.

The sharing session introduced departments that are closely related to Data Engineering, especially the Data Engineering department and SAP Operations.

### 📊 Data Engineering & SAP Experience

One of the most valuable parts of the visit was learning about PPG’s data lake architecture. Data from sources such as Oracle, SAP, business applications, files, and logs is collected and processed through several layers, including Bronze, Silver, Gold, and Curated layers.

This helped me understand how raw data must be ingested, cleaned, transformed, and prepared before it can be used for reporting, dashboard development, and business decision-making. Tools such as Azure Data Factory, Azure Databricks, Delta Lake, Python, SQL, and Power BI were also introduced.

I also learned that SAP Operations plays an important role in managing business processes and system changes. Every change request must go through review, design, testing, user acceptance testing, production release, and ticket closure to ensure smooth and accurate system operations.

### 🏭 Inventory Risk Management Case Study

The final session focused on an inventory management case study related to Recoverable Assets (RA) and Magna Carta (MC). The project aimed to identify excess inventory, flag write-down candidates, calculate financial impact, detect stockout risks, and support decision-making.

This case study helped me understand how data engineering solutions can be used to solve real business problems and reduce financial risk.

### 🌟 Overall Reflection

Overall, this industry visit was a valuable experience because it connected classroom learning with real industry practices. It showed me that data engineering is not only about technical skills, but also about understanding business needs and creating useful solutions that support decision-making.



## 🤖 Reflection: iZeno Singapore Industry Talk

On the 13th of May, I attended an industry talk by iZeno Singapore. The session was delivered by Arissa Wong and Jovinka Hartanto, who shared company insights, working experiences, and technical knowledge from real industry projects.

### 💡 What I Gained

This session helped me understand the role of technology consulting companies in solving real business problems. I learned that companies like iZeno help clients build systems, automate processes, and improve business operations through data and AI-related solutions.

It also gave me a clearer picture of how data engineering and artificial intelligence are applied in real production environments.

### 📈 Production Pipeline Experience

One of the most interesting parts of the session was the explanation of a real production pipeline. The pipeline included several stages such as source systems, ingestion layer, Bronze layer, Silver layer, Gold layer, AI or LLM layer, BI and analytics, and monitoring or alerts.

This helped me understand that industry-level pipelines are usually more complex than academic projects. In real industry environments, more attention must be given to data quality, reliability, scalability, monitoring, and business impact.

### 🧠 AI & Automation Experience

The session also introduced an AI-powered news intelligence platform as a case study. Through this example, I learned that AI can be used to automate news ingestion, summarization, classification, and entity extraction.

This showed me how AI can reduce repetitive manual work and allow analysts to focus more on insights and decision-making.

### 🔧 Suggested Improvements

To be better prepared for the industry, I should continue improving my skills in data pipelines, cloud platforms, AI tools, monitoring, and problem-solving. I also need to understand how technical solutions can create value for businesses.

### 🌟 Overall Reflection

Overall, the iZeno industry talk was eye-opening because it connected classroom knowledge with real-world applications. It motivated me to build stronger foundations in data engineering, artificial intelligence, cloud technologies, and business problem-solving skills.

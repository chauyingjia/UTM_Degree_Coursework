# Artificial Intelligence Reflection

## Course Overview

This course introduced the foundations of artificial intelligence, including problem solving, search, reasoning, and the way AI systems are designed from a clear problem definition. The work in this folder includes the course assignments and the project development files such as the proposal, progress update, proof of concept, and individual assignment.

## Reflection

This course helped me understand that AI is not only about advanced models, but also about how a problem is framed, analysed, and evaluated. The assignments trained me to think more carefully about the logic behind a solution before trying to build it. The project files also showed me that AI work needs planning, iteration, and clear documentation, not just implementation.

Through the proposal, progress report, and proof of concept, I learned how to turn an idea into a more structured project. That process improved my ability to explain a technical idea, test whether it was realistic, and refine it based on feedback. It also strengthened my confidence in handling AI-related tasks that require both analytical thinking and practical development.

## Overall Learning

Overall, this course gave me a stronger foundation in AI thinking and problem solving. It helped me see how AI connects with real applications and encouraged me to approach future AI topics with more structure and curiosity.
